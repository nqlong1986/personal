About Force Units

Force measures the interaction that changes an object's motion. The SI unit of force is the Newton (N). Other commonly used units include Pound-force (lbf), Kilogram-force (kgf), Dyne, and Kilonewton (kN).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Newton?

One Newton is the force required to accelerate a one-kilogram mass at one meter per second squared.

How many Newtons are in one Pound-force?

1 Pound-force = 4.448221615 Newtons.

How many Dynes are in one Newton?

1 Newton = 100,000 Dynes.
