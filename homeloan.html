How to Use the Home Loan Calculator

Enter the amount you wish to borrow, your annual interest rate, and the loan repayment period. The calculator instantly estimates your monthly mortgage payment using the standard amortization formula.

Why Use a Mortgage Calculator?

A home loan calculator helps homebuyers understand their monthly financial commitment before applying for a mortgage. It also shows how much interest will be paid over the lifetime of the loan, making it easier to compare loan options and plan your budget.
