What is Inflation?

Inflation is the gradual increase in prices over time, reducing the purchasing power of money. For example, if inflation averages 3% annually, an item costing $100 today may cost about $134 after 10 years.

Inflation Formula

Future Value = Present Value × (1 + Inflation Rate)Years
