How Does a Personal Loan Calculator Work?

A personal loan calculator uses the standard loan amortization formula to estimate your monthly payment based on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment period. This helps borrowers compare loans and plan their monthly budget before applying.

Formula Used

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

Symbol Meaning
P Loan Amount
r Monthly Interest Rate
n Total Monthly Payments

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is a personal loan calculator?

A personal loan calculator is an online tool that estimates your monthly payment (EMI), total interest, and total repayment amount based on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term.

2. How is the monthly payment calculated?

Monthly payments are calculated using the standard loan amortization formula, which considers the principal amount, annual interest rate, and repayment period.
