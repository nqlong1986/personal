What is PSI?

PSI (Pounds per Square Inch) is a pressure unit commonly used in the United States for measuring tire pressure, compressed air, hydraulic systems, and industrial equipment.

What is Bar?

Bar is a metric pressure unit equal to 100,000 Pascals. It is widely used in engineering, weather forecasting, and industrial applications.

What is Atmospheric Pressure?

Standard atmospheric pressure equals 1 atm or 101,325 Pascals. Atmospheric pressure changes depending on altitude and weather conditions.
