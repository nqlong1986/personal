Frequently Asked Questions

What is a time zone?

A time zone is a geographic region that observes the same standard time.

Does this converter adjust for daylight saving?

Yes. Supported browsers automatically apply Daylight Saving Time rules.

Can I convert UTC to local time?

Yes. Simply choose UTC as the source and your preferred destination time zone.
